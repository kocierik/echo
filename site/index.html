It comes to you, rarely

When someone you know starts talking, what you owe each other. Ten minutes before a meeting, what to know. Promises on their day.

At most three a day, never between 22 and 8, each kind can be turned off. While it listens, it’s on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.

“Ehi Echo, every morning at 7, tell me if it rains in Milan.”

A routine: daily, weekdays, weekly or monthly. It tells you always, only when it’s true, or only what’s new.

Private by design

Does it record everything?
Only speech, from the first word to the silence. A long press on its button mutes it.
Where does the audio go?
Over encrypted Bluetooth to your phone, then to Echo’s server to be written out. It is deleted a day later.
And without my phone?
It holds about 36 hours per GB, encrypted with a key that never leaves it.
Does it act on its own?
Only when you ask. Sending, changing or deleting waits for your Confirm.
Can I take my data?
All of it, as one file. Each phone has its own key, and you can revoke it.
