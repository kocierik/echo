Before you wear it

Does it record everything?
Only speech, from the first word to the silence. A long press on its button mutes it.
Where does the audio go?
To your phone, then to Echo’s server to be written out. It is deleted a day later.
Does it act on its own?
Only when you ask. “Ehi Echo, segnami il dentista giovedì” puts it in your calendar.
