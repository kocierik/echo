before you wear it?

does it record everything?
Only speech, first word to silence. A long press on its button mutes it.
where does the audio go?
To your phone, then to Echo’s server to be written out. The audio is deleted a day later.
which languages does it hear?
Italian and English; others around you, translated.
does it act on its own?
Only when you ask. “Ehi Echo, segnami il dentista giovedì” puts it in your calendar.

what happens to a sentence?

  1. the pendant hears speech, lights its LED
  2. your iPhone takes it over Bluetooth
  3. Echo’s server writes it out, finds people, promises and dates
  4. your memory Today, People and Ask
