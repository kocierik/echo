From a sentence to an answer

    From the first word to the silence.

  2. Your iPhone passes it on

    Over Bluetooth, to Echo’s server, to be written out.

  3. It finds what matters

    People, promises and dates, from the words alone.

    In Ask, typed or said, or on Today and People.

Before you wear it

Does it record everything?
Only speech. A long press on its button mutes it.
Which languages does it hear?
Italian and English; others around you, translated.
Does it act on its own?
Only when you ask. “Ehi Echo, segnami il dentista giovedì” puts it in your calendar.
